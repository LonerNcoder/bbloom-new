# Welcome to BBloom

As AI continues to integrate into nearly every aspect of the online world, I’m excited to contribute to the space for book lovers. This site is currently in active development, aiming to provide writers and book readers with a free platform to write, read, and build a growing community without incurring costs.

## How will the site offer free AI services to users?

The site serves as a frontend to test my custom **APIDistributor** model, which efficiently load-balances requests to keep operational costs under a dollar.
